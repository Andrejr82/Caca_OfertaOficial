# Product Vision

## Product

Caça Oferta Oficial is a web platform for affiliate offer operations. It helps a small operator register offers, score them, generate channel-ready messages, publish approved offers to Telegram, prepare Instagram and WhatsApp content manually, track affiliate links and record sales.

## Official Channels

- Brand: Caça Oferta Oficial

- Telegram: Caça Oferta Oficial
- Telegram URL: https://t.me/caca_ofertaoficial
- WhatsApp: Caça Oferta Oficial

## Audience

The first users are non-technical affiliate operators who need a simple dashboard and safe automation. The interface must be practical, readable and hard to misuse.

## MVP

- Supabase Auth login.
- Protected dashboard.
- Offer CRUD.
- Offer score from business rules.
- Message generation for Telegram, Instagram and WhatsApp.
- Telegram Bot API publication.
- Tracked link generation by channel.
- Manual sales and commission records.
- Integration settings without exposing secrets.
